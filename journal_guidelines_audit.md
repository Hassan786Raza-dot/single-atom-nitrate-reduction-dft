# Journal-guideline audit

## Candidate target

The manuscript is best aligned with **Communications Chemistry** or **Journal of Materials Chemistry A** as a specialised computational/materials study. Nature Communications states that primary research should represent high-quality advances of significance to specialists. Communications Chemistry states that research papers should provide important chemical insight and satisfy Nature Portfolio technical-validity and ethical standards. Journal of Materials Chemistry A distinguishes Full Papers containing original scientific work from short Communications and requires a materials contribution relevant to energy and sustainability.

## Requirements relevant to this project

The manuscript must contain complete standard sections, clear claims supported by the data, reproducible methods, accessible data/code where possible, and an honest statement of limitations. ACS guidance additionally recommends embedding figures, charts, tables, schemes, and equations near the point of discussion, supplying supporting information separately, and providing complete reference information. The RSC guidance emphasises article-type selection, machine-readable data where applicable, appropriate error/uncertainty reporting, and complete experimental/computational conditions.

## Current compliance status

The manuscript has a title, abstract, introduction, methods, results/discussion, limitations, conclusions, references, an embedded convergence figure, and separate supporting information. It does not yet meet the evidentiary standard for a completed catalyst-ranking paper because the full converged SAC matrix and reaction pathways are absent. The audit therefore expands the manuscript as a transparent computational benchmark and workflow paper rather than inventing activity claims. The final manuscript must keep this distinction explicit.

## Requirements re-checked from official pages

Nature Communications Article guidance states that the abstract should be no more than 200 words and contain no references. It recommends an Introduction followed by Results, Discussion where appropriate, and Methods, with topical subheadings for Results and Methods. It gives approximately 5,000 words for the main text excluding Abstract, Methods, References, and figure legends, and approximately 10 display items as a general guide. It also requires competing-interests and author-contribution statements before publication. Source data should contain the numerical data underlying graphs and charts, and custom code central to the claims should be available to editors and reviewers.

ACS initial-submission guidance requires standard sections and complete numbered and labelled components. Figures, charts, tables, schemes, and equations should be embedded at the point of relevance; references should be complete and include titles; and Supporting Information should be supplied separately. ACS strongly encourages public availability of the research data underlying the article.

RSC Journal of Materials Chemistry A guidance distinguishes Full Papers containing original unpublished scientific work from Communications and review-type articles. It recommends using figures, tables, and equations for clarity and brevity rather than unnecessary duplication and emphasises machine-readable data and complete computational or experimental conditions.

The current manuscript now follows the strict citation architecture: zero citations in the abstract; 25 references with all 25 cited in the main text; sequential individual numeric citations; explicit references to Tables 1–4, Figure 1, Equations 1–2, and Supporting Information; and bounded novelty statements in the abstract, Introduction, Results, strengths/limitations, future recommendations, and conclusion.
