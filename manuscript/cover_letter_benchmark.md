# Cover letter draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **“[Insert benchmark-focused title]”**, for publication as a computational methodology/reproducibility contribution.

The manuscript presents an auditable periodic-DFT benchmark for a proposed 30-model single-atom-catalyst screen for nitrate-to-ammonia electroreduction. Its central contribution is an evidence-gated workflow that separates valid starting structures, numerical diagnostics, executable calculations, accepted chemical observables, electrochemical validity, stability, and provenance. The repository contains 96 audited starting structures, reproducible GPAW/ASE diagnostics, a machine-readable production manifest, raw diagnostic records, validation scripts, source-data pathways, and the manuscript Supporting Information.

The work is deliberately bounded. It does not claim a best catalyst, nitrate adsorption free energy, reaction mechanism, free-energy pathway, limiting potential, ammonia selectivity, or catalytic ranking. Instead, it demonstrates why those claims require observable-based convergence, magnetic-state sampling, charged-nitrate treatment, solvation and potential sensitivity, complete reaction-network calculations, stability analysis, and uncertainty quantification. This boundary is a principal scientific result rather than an omission concealed by presentation.

The manuscript contains no citations in the abstract, uses 25 references cited in the main text, explicitly cross-references its tables, figure, equations, and Supporting Information, and provides a public repository for independent inspection. All conclusions are restricted to the archived evidence, and failed or incomplete calculations are retained and labelled diagnostic-only.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved its submission. We believe it will be useful to researchers developing reproducible computational catalysis workflows and to readers who need practical criteria for determining when high-throughput electrocatalyst screening is ready for interpretation.

Yours sincerely,
